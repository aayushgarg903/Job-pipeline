# Pitch and field kit

Everything we show judges and everything we send out to employers and the Department, for Kaushal Setu (SIH 2026, PS 26134). Owner: F (Domain / Pitch).

| File | What it is | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| [pitch-outline.md](./pitch-outline.md) | 12-slide idea-submission deck: on-slide text, speaker notes and card-style visuals, mapped to SIH's template blocks, with references | building the deck, or compressing it to SIH's 6-block format |
| [demo-script.md](./demo-script.md) | The 6-minute live demo, word for word: what to click, what to say, a fallback line for every step | rehearsing, and on judging day |
| [judge-qa.md](./judge-qa.md) | 25 hard judge questions with short answers and an owner for each area | Q&A prep; the last 20 minutes before going in |
| [employer-survey.md](./employer-survey.md) | The ≤ 6-minute employer survey in English and Marathi, ready for Google Forms, with DPDP consent, a field map to `survey_response`, and WhatsApp outreach for MCCIA, NIMA and ITI placement cells | setting up the form and sending it out (target ≥ 25 responses by 18 Oct) |
| [data-request-letter.md](./data-request-letter.md) | Formal letter to MSSDS / DSEEI asking for district ITI seats, trainer and equipment registers, placement tracer data and Mahaswayam vacancies, with what we give back and our safeguards | sending in M0 week (by 4 Oct), so data can arrive by 11 Oct |

## Rules for anything in this folder

- Numbers come from [Architecture.md §5](../Architecture.md) with the source named, or they're marked *(illustrative)*. Don't add a statistic you can't point to.
- Demo data carries the `SPECIMEN` watermark in every screenshot. Never crop it.
- Voice: plain words, people not percentages, say who said it, be honest about what we don't know, end with a next step ([Design.md §1](../Design.md)).
- Placeholders to fill before anything goes out: `[TEAM NAME]`, `[College]`, `[City]`, `[TEAM EMAIL]`, `[PHONE]`, `[FORM LINK]`, names and dates.
