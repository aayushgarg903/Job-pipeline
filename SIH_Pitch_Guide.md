## The 8-Step Pitch Flow

### 1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:35)
- **What to Show:** The main dashboard as a District Official.
- **The Pitch:** Explain that Kaushal Setu is built on real-world data (job postings, employer surveys). Point out the `SPECIMEN` watermark immediately to build trust. This shows the judges you are honest about what is live data versus demo data.

### 2. The Honest Map (0:35 - 1:15)
- **What to Show:** The Maharashtra map highlighting the gap between training and jobs.
- **The Pitch:** Hover over a high-data district (Pune) and a low-data district (Gadchiroli). Emphasize that unlike other tools that fake data, your app admits when it has "low signal" and falls back to Udyam business registrations instead. Judges love transparency.

### 3. The Comparison Table (1:15 - 2:05)
- **What to Show:** Compare an Electrician course in Nashik vs. Pune side-by-side.
- **The Pitch:** Show that while they look identical on paper, local Nashik employers specifically need "solar PV installation" and "EV charging." Point out that without your tool, a student in Nashik misses out on the solar job right down their street.

### 4. The "Curriculum PR" (2:05 - 2:50)
- **What to Show:** Click "Open Curriculum PR".
- **The Pitch:** This is the wow factor. Explain that you don't just give the government a static chart; you give them a GitHub-style "Pull Request" for the syllabus. Show the module-level fixes (e.g., Add solar PV, 30 hours) that are directly endorsed by real local employers.

### 5. District Training Plan (2:50 - 3:50)
- **What to Show:** Open the FY27 plan, run the optimizer, and generate the PDF.
- **The Pitch:** Explain that the mathematical optimizer (HiGHS) respects real-world constraints (budget, trainers, equipment) to maximize *actual student placements*. Show that an official can review it and click "Sign this plan" to instantly generate an A4 PDF for their files.

### 6. The Radar (3:50 - 4:25)
- **What to Show:** The tech trends radar (e.g., Battery management systems).
- **The Pitch:** Show the gap between global tech curves and local ITI teaching. This proves your tool acts as an early-warning system for the government to stay ahead of industry shifts.

### 7. The Job-Seeker in Marathi (4:25 - 5:20)
- **What to Show:** Switch your browser to mobile view (Phone emulator) and open the `/me` portal. 
- **The Pitch:** Paste a natural sentence in Marathi (e.g., "I know house wiring, where can I go?"). Show how Gemini parses the Marathi text, understands their skills, and immediately recommends free SWAYAM courses to bridge their gap to getting hired.

### 8. The Evidence Drawer (5:20 - 5:50)
- **What to Show:** Click on any health score number on the dashboard.
- **The Pitch:** Close the pitch by showing the "Evidence Drawer" sliding in. Prove that *nothing* is a black box. Every metric is backed by a specific sentence extracted by AI from a real employer survey or job posting.
